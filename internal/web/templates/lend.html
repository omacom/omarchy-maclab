Lend a Mac

test machines only

Lend only a Test machine: a Mac with nothing on it, in Omarchy or in macOS, that you cannot afford to lose or to have read. A Mac you use every day is not welcome. No software checks this: your typed yes at the Mac covers it.

what the lab may and may not do

The lab may
Qualification (every Mac starts here)Restart the Mac into test kernels, the M team's own included, and back; run its own tests; collect hardware facts, logs and test output; see the screen and the live journal while a job or a Lease holds the Mac. Who: Maintainers, and M team members holding a Lease.
Full (you choose it, on the Mac)Also a root shell, their own scripts, staged Mesa.
NeverA Hard reset: a hung Mac waits for its own watchdog, then for you. Anything outside the Availability window or on battery. Publishing its reports unless you turn that on. Showing your handle to the public.

A test kernel has full control of the machine, files included. Installing a Release and rolling back is not offered on a Volunteer's Mac. Joining, pausing, changing the window or the Access level, and leaving are posted to the Lab group on Signal, where the M team is, under the Mac's name, never yours. Nothing is sent to you about Leases: My Macs shows who holds one and until when. You set the window, pause, choose the Access level, read every record and leave at any time.

A Mac that is in another lab: the Join command names that lab and asks once. On yes the Mac leaves it properly, then joins here at Qualification with publishing off; the window it had is kept.
