Everything published here is CC0: use it for anything. Hidden and deleted reports are never included.

reports.jsonevery report exactly as uploaded, with its link and upload time
reports.csvone row per report: Mac, stack, versions and pass/fail/skip counts
checks.csvone row per check result, with its outcome, feature, layer, expected states and any benchmark score
matrix.jsonthe compatibility matrix, with each cell's agreement per machine
benchmarks.jsonbenchmark scores per Mac model and stack/version: median, range, machines and runs
aurora.jsonthe Aurora feature-support table per chip generation, from Verified runs, with each cell's runs and the catalogue's expected states

In the exports "tester" means Verified, as in tester_machines: the old names are kept so that what reads the exports keeps working.
